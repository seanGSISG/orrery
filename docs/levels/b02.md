# B2 Loose Hours

Map: `b02.svg`. Ticket: #29. Shared numbers and assumptions: `README.md`.

## Intent

The second level teaches jumping and checkpoints, with trapdoors and crumbling tiles. The player learns each one in a safe place, then crosses a wide hall where the floor is the puzzle. The hall has no fixed path. Slow players go round on solid panels. Fast players go straight across the loose ones.

Flavour: a clock workshop floor with brass hatches, some of them worn loose.

## Layout

| Area | Size | Height | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start court | 20 × 8 m | 0 m | |
| Jump yard | 38 × 17 m | 0 m | A channel 3 m wide and 1 m deep crosses the whole yard. It has a floor and ramps at both ends, so a missed jump costs 2 s. One block 4 × 4 m at +1 m |
| Gate | 16 × 3 m | 0 m | The only way from the yard to the hall. Checkpoint 1 |
| Trapdoor hall | 40 × 16 m, 40 panels of 4 × 4 m | 0 m | 27 solid, 7 trapdoors, 3 crumbling, 3 holes. Solid panels make a safe way at the west end and at the east side |
| Undercroft | 20 × 8 m | −4 m | Under the west half of the hall. Three steps of 1 m lead back up to the yard |
| Terrace | 24 × 5 m | +1 m | One step up from the hall. Checkpoint 2 |
| Landing and finish deck | 12 × 4 m, 14 × 7 m | +4 m, +5 m | |

Three ways go from the terrace to the landing:

- **West stair:** three solid pads at +2, +3 and +4 m with 2 m gaps, then a bridge. Safe and long.
- **Centre pillars:** two pillars 2 m wide at +2.2 and +3.3 m. Three precise jumps with rises of 1.2, 1.1 and 0.7 m. The shortest way.
- **East sheet:** a ramp of crumbling tiles 6 × 10 m from +1 to +4 m, then a crumbling bridge 10 m long. The fastest way. The player must not stop.

## Routes

**First run (white).** Jump the channel through the first gem. Jump onto the block for the second gem. Pass the gate. In the hall, go west along the solid south row, roll fast over one trapdoor for a gem, then follow solid panels east. Step onto one crumbling tile for a gem and step back. Take the gem on the east island. Jump up to the terrace, climb the west stair, jump for the last gem and roll onto the finish. Length 192 m, 8 jumps.

**Expert (dashed).** After the gate, cut straight to the trapdoor gem. Then run the middle row east in one line: crumbling tile with gem, trapdoor, a jump over a 4 m hole, island gem. Jump to the terrace and take the east sheet at full speed. Length 131 m, 6 jumps.

## Gems: 6

- 2 in the yard: one above the channel (a jump collects it), one on the block (a jump onto a step).
- 3 in the hall: one on a west trapdoor, one on a centre crumbling tile, one on a solid island in the east. Each loose floor type holds one gem, so the player must touch each type.
- 1 above the landing at +1.2 m. All three ways up meet under it, so no way is forced.

## Clockwork Egg

The egg is in a nook at the east end of the undercroft, behind a pillar. The trapdoor straight ahead of the gate, next to the first hole, drops the marble straight into the nook. A player who falls through any west trapdoor lands in the undercroft, sees the steps to the west and can look east instead. The level's main mistake is the door to its secret.

## Hazards and objects

Start pad, finish pad, 6 gems, 1 egg, 2 checkpoints, 7 trapdoors, 3 crumbling tiles in the hall, the crumbling sheet and bridge.

## Checkpoints

- **Checkpoint 1** at the gate, after the safe jumps and before the hall.
- **Checkpoint 2** on the terrace, after the hall and before the climb.

A fall in the west half of the hall lands in the undercroft and costs about 6 s. A fall in the east half goes into the void and returns the marble to checkpoint 1 with its gems. Both results teach what a checkpoint does.

| Gold | | 32 × 1.15 | 37 s |

Speed checks: a 4 m trapdoor needs 4 ÷ 0.5 = 8 m/s. The 4 m hole needs 4 ÷ 0.75 = 5.3 m/s. The crumbling sheet and bridge are 20 m and each tile holds for 0.5 s, so any speed above 8 m/s is safe.

## Why it is fun

- The hall shows the whole problem at once. The player reads the floor and plans a line.
- Speed changes the map: at 8 m/s the trapdoors become floor. The player feels this the first time a hatch drops behind the marble.
- A fall is a short trip through the undercroft, not a restart.
- The three climbs give a safe way, a precise way and a fast way from the same spot.
