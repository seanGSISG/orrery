# Ascent 3: Sky Harbour

Map: `ascent-z3.svg`. Ticket: #33. Shared numbers and rules: `README.md`. Heights: 300 to 500 m. The zone starts on the spire cap, where zone 2 ends, and ends at the "to be continued" gate.

## Intent

Above the tower, the roofs of the harbour quarter float between a flotilla of moored airships. The zone mixes every M1 object and asks for longer chains of moves between rest ledges. The backs of the airships and the big roofs are wide floors. The zone has six parts, and each part has two ways up.

## Routes

**First run (white).** Roofs, the Courier, roofs again, the gangplanks, the cargo lifts, then the inside of the Flagship. The player waits for nine platforms and never rolls on a loose floor.

**Expert (dashed).**

1. Roofs to Roof 2, then the first mooring rope: 0.6 m wide, 16.6 m long, 25°, straight to the Harbourmaster roof.
2. The mast side: two vents and no platform to wait for.
3. The Lamplighter: four vents and the roll along its back.
4. The three mooring ropes of the flotilla. They need a run-up: at 30° to 35° the marble gains speed slowly.
5. The crow's nests and the yardarm.
6. The outside of the Flagship at full speed over the loose slates.

## Gems

Ascent has no gems.

## Clockwork Eggs (2 of 10)

1. **Cargo crate.** A crate hangs under the Tender at +297.8 m, below the zone start. The player sees it from the spire ramp at the top of zone 2 and from the loose planks. Drop from the planks onto the crate. A vent on the crate lifts the marble back to the gondola deck.
2. **Fin tip.** At the top of the Flagship, the mast and the gate are in the middle. The tail fin stands to the east. Three steps with rises of 1.2 to 1.3 m lead from the east end of the top to the fin tip at +483 m, where the egg sits. The player must turn away from the gate to find it.

## Hazards and objects

16 Steam Vents (plus 1 on the crate), 5 bumpers, 3 pendulums, 1 turning gear, 10 moving platforms (4 gangways, 3 gangplanks, 3 cargo lifts), 4 strips of Crumbling Tiles, 5 narrow beams (4 mooring ropes, 1 yardarm), 2 eggs, the gate. No carried power-ups.

## Why it is fun

- The airship backs and the big roofs are the widest floors in the climb. After two tight zones the player gets room to roll.
- Each part pairs a slow safe way with platforms against a fast way with no waits. The player can swap sides at every rest ledge.
- The flotilla ropes cross open sky from ship to ship. They are the hardest moves in M1 and they are in view the whole time.
- The two eggs sit at the bottom and the top of the zone. Both need a move that goes against the climb.
- The gate at the top faces the next zone, so the slice ends with a view of what comes next.
