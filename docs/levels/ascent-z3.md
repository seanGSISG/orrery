# Ascent 3: Sky Harbour

Map: `ascent-z3.svg`. Ticket: #33. Shared numbers and rules: `README.md`. Heights: 300 to 500 m. The zone starts on the spire cap, where zone 2 ends, and ends at the "to be continued" gate.

## Intent

Above the tower, the roofs of the harbour quarter float between a flotilla of moored airships. The zone mixes every M1 object and asks for longer chains of moves between rest ledges. The backs of the airships and the big roofs are wide floors. The zone has six parts, and each part has two ways up.

## Routes

**First run (white).** Roofs, the Courier, roofs again, the gangplanks, the cargo lifts, then the inside of the Flagship. The player waits for nine platforms and never rolls on a loose floor.

**Expert (dashed).**

1. Roofs to Roof 2, then the first mooring rope: 0.6 m wide, 16.6 m long, 25°, straight to the Harbourmaster roof.
2. The mast side: two vents and no platform to wait for.
3. The Lamplighter: four vents and the roll along its back.
4. The three mooring ropes of the flotilla. They need a run-up: at 30° to 35° the marble gains speed slowly.
5. The crow's nests and the yardarm.
6. The outside of the Flagship at full speed over the loose slates.

## Gems

Ascent has no gems.

## Clockwork Eggs (2 of 10)

1. **Cargo crate.** A crate hangs under the Tender at +297.8 m, below the zone start. The player sees it from the spire ramp at the top of zone 2 and from the loose planks. Drop from the planks onto the crate. A vent on the crate lifts the marble back to the gondola deck.
2. **Fin tip.** At the top of the Flagship, the mast and the gate are in the middle. The tail fin stands to the east. Three steps with rises of 1.2 to 1.3 m lead from the east end of the top to the fin tip at +483 m, where the egg sits. The player must turn away from the gate to find it.

## Hazards and objects

16 Steam Vents (plus 1 on the crate), 5 bumpers, 3 pendulums, 1 turning gear, 10 moving platforms (4 gangways, 3 gangplanks, 3 cargo lifts), 4 strips of Crumbling Tiles, 5 narrow beams (4 mooring ropes, 1 yardarm), 2 eggs, the gate. No carried power-ups.

## Why it is fun

- The airship backs and the big roofs are the widest floors in the climb. After two tight zones the player gets room to roll.
- Each part pairs a slow safe way with platforms against a fast way with no waits. The player can swap sides at every rest ledge.
- The flotilla ropes cross open sky from ship to ship. They are the hardest moves in M1 and they are in view the whole time.
- The two eggs sit at the bottom and the top of the zone. Both need a move that goes against the climb.
- The gate at the top faces the next zone, so the slice ends with a view of what comes next.

## As built

Level script: `levels/ascent/zone_3.py`. Harbour kit: `kit/harbour.py`. The script holds the layout as named constants, and most places follow from the ones before them: read the script for the numbers. Plan: x east, y north. Parts 1 to 3 climb north from the quay (y 25 to 118). Part 4 runs north from the Signal roof to Skiff 1, then west to Skiff 2 and the Tanker. Parts 5 and 6 run west from the Tanker to the Flagship (x about -142 to -70, y about 120 to 152).

The zone uses the town finishes of zone 1 (#31). The brass fittings of the ships (mooring cones, tail caps, cabin window frames, deck-edge bands, spinners and propeller hubs) take zone 1's `brass_polished`. Polished brass is only on trim and curved parts; the kerbs are kit `brass`, and the propeller blades are `varnish`. The harbour adds three finishes: `envelope`, `varnish` and `rope`.

An envelope has 24 gores. The middle of each gore stands 2.5 % of the radius out of the polygon of its girders, and the fabric sags 2 % of the radius between two ring frames, 3.2 m apart, with a crease at each frame. A seam of `rope`, 0.1 m wide, runs along each girder and round each frame. Posts stand under a deck on a ship's back every 4.8 m; where the envelope is narrower than the deck, they stand further in, so each one ends in the fabric. Ropes, rigging and mast braces are 0.07 m thick or more, so they do not break into dots at range. A mast that hangs in the air ends below in an iron point. The east half of the Flagship's top has a skylight and two capstans, off both routes.

The route bot drives both routes to the gate (`just test-levels zone_3`, 2026-11-21, after the style review fixes): first run **214.1 s**, expert **128.0 s**. Each route starts with a stop on the start pad, and the expert route stops again before the first rope (see the handover below). Par 321.2 s and gold 147.3 s come from `just suggest-times zone_3`. The bot is slower than the estimates (152 s and 73 s): it slows to 3.5 m/s at each sharp turn and it waits for each mover's window.

The Tender way of part 1 is on neither route. A temporary first-run route through it (planks, gondola deck, sponson vent, fin, deckhouse, stern steps, gangway B) reached the gate in 224.0 s; the bot needs a straight run of 1.5 m before the jump onto the deckhouse and before the jump from stern step 1.

`just perf zone_3` (RTX 5090, 2026-11-21, after the merge of the zone 1 art pass): Medium 1080p p99 0.47 ms and 1058 MB VRAM (gates 3.0 ms, 4096 MB); Ultra 4K p99 4.05 ms (gate 16.7 ms).

**Rules found while building (vents and the bot).**

- A vent throw needs lift = rise + 2.5 to 3 m when the next floor's edge is 2.2 to 2.5 m past the column's edge. With less, the marble meets the side of the floor and drops back into the column, where it hovers: the bot cannot push in the air with no speed ("stuck" at the column top).
- Enter a vent from a straight approach of 1.5 m or more at speed 3, with the approach point 1.2 m before the vent's middle.
- The marble climbs a beam of 30° for about 24 m and of 32° for about 22 m from a run-up at full speed. At 35° it stalls. Keep the run-up straight and in line with the beam; after a sharp turn the bot swings 0.8 m off the line, so slow it (`speed 2.5`, then `speed 3`) before a 0.6 m rope.
- For a 0.6 m rope that a turn leads onto, stop the bot (`wait 0`) in line with the rope, then send it through a point on the line about 3 m before the foot. The marble climbs the 25° first rope from a stop 5.9 m before the foot. Without the stop, a start that differed by 0.3 m made the bot fall from the rope in most drives.
- A marble that rolls off a turning gear can drop between two teeth: the gap is 0.4 m deep into the disc and 0.7 m wide at the tips. Put the next floor a step below the gear (0.55 m here), reaching in under its teeth.
- `kit.marker` names path points with three digits above 100 points. The first-run route of this zone has more than 100 points.
- The bot counts its 300 s limit from its own first input, so a climb can give a bot to each zone in turn (`route_bot.gd`). Zones 2 and 3 take about 394 s.
- Shot names share one namespace with the level's objects: `kit.shot("rigging")` fails when an object is named `rigging`.
- To see where the bot fails, copy `game/harness/route_bot.gd`, add a `print` in `_steer()` or at `falls += 1`, run `just test-levels zone_3`, then restore the file. `uv run --quiet --project tools/harness orrery-harness teleport --velocity vx vy vz x y z` (Godot axes: y up, z = -north) probes one throw.
