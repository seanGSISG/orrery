# Ascent 3: Sky Harbour

Map: `ascent-z3.svg`. Ticket: #33. Shared numbers and assumptions: `README.md`. Heights: 100 to 170 m. The zone starts on the spire cap, where zone 2 ends, and ends at the "to be continued" gate.

## Intent

Above the tower, the roofs of the harbour quarter float between three moored airships. The zone mixes every M1 object and asks for longer chains of moves between rest ledges. The backs of the airships are wide curved floors. The zone has three parts, and each part has two ways up.

## Layout

**First part, +100 to +122 m**

- **West, roofs:** two steps. Roof 1: a 32° slope, 9.4 m, to a ridge at +108 m. Ridge vent, lift to +115.5 m. Roof 2 at +115 m, 8 m wide, with two bumpers. Three dormer steps to +118 m. Gangway A: a 3 m platform from +118 to +122 m, period 8 s.
- **East, the Tender:** loose planks (Crumbling Tiles), 4.4 m. Gondola deck at +101 m, 14 m long. Deck vent, lift to +109 m. Fin platform at +108.5 m and a short ramp to the top of the airship at +113 m, 11 m wide. Gangway B: a 3 m platform from +113 to +122 m, period 10 s.
- **Harbourmaster roof, +122 m.** 10 m wide. Rest ledge. Both ways meet here.

**Second part, +122 to +144 m**

- **West, the Courier:** three tile steps to the gondola deck at +126 m. Three ramps inside the hull (30 m in total) with one pendulum. The top of the airship at +140 m, 14 m wide. Rest ledge. Gangplank C: a 3 m platform from +140 to +144 m, period 10 s.
- **East, the mast:** roof vent, lift to +129 m. Loose shingles (Crumbling Tiles), 5.4 m, at +128.5 m. Two steps. A flat propeller gear, 6 m across, at +131.5 m. Vent, lift to +140 m. Mast platform at +139.5 m. Rest ledge. Three steps to +144 m.

**Last part, the Flagship, +144 to +170 m**

- Gondola deck at +144 m, 22 m long. Rest ledge. Both ways meet here.
- **Inside:** two hull ramps (18.7 m and 17.7 m, 15°) to +154 m. A vent lifts the marble through the top hatch to +162 m.
- **Outside:** a ramp of loose shingles on the tail fin (11 m, 29°), a second ramp (10 m, 32°) to the stern gallery at +154.6 m, and a vent with lift to +161 m. Two bumpers.
- The top of the Flagship at +162 m, 22 m wide. A spiral ramp round the mast (25 m, 18°) climbs to the gate at +170 m.

## Routes

**First run (white).** West roofs, the Courier, then the inside of the Flagship. The player waits for three platforms and never rolls on a loose floor.

**Expert (dashed).** West roofs to Roof 2. Then the mooring rope: a beam 0.6 m wide, 16.6 m long, 25°, from Roof 2 straight to the Harbourmaster roof. It skips the dormer steps and gangway A. Then the east way to the mast, which has no platform to wait for. Then the outside of the Flagship at full speed over the loose shingles.

## Gems

Ascent has no gems.

## Clockwork Eggs (2 of 10)

1. **Cargo crate.** A crate hangs under the Tender at +97.8 m. The player sees it from the Mainspring ramp in zone 2 and from the loose planks. Drop from the planks onto the crate. A vent on the crate lifts the marble back to the gondola deck.
2. **Fin tip.** At the top of the Flagship, the gate is to the west. The tail fin stands to the east. Three steps with rises of 1.2 to 1.3 m lead from the east end of the top to the fin tip at +164 m, where the egg sits. The player must turn away from the gate to find it.

## Hazards and objects

6 Steam Vents (plus 1 on the crate), 4 bumpers, 1 pendulum, 1 turning gear, 3 moving platforms, 3 strips of Crumbling Tiles, 2 eggs, the gate. No carried power-ups.

## Falls and rest ledges

| Fall from | Lands on | Height lost |
|---|---|---|
| Roof 2, dormer steps, mooring rope | Roof 1 or the spire cap | 7 to 22 m |
| The Tender | Zone 2: Mainspring ramp or Bell ledge | 10 to 40 m |
| The Courier or gangplank C | Harbourmaster roof, or Roof 2 | 4 to 25 m |
| Mast steps, propeller gear | Top of the Tender | 17 to 31 m |
| Outside of the Flagship | Mast platform | 5 to 21 m |
| Top of the Flagship | Flagship deck or the Courier | 18 to 22 m |

Each airship hangs over a wide surface, so most falls in this zone end one part lower. Rest ledges: spire cap, Harbourmaster roof, Courier top, mast platform, Flagship deck. No checkpoints. With Assist, one checkpoint on the spire cap.

## Why it is fun

- The airship backs are the widest floors in the climb. After two tight zones the player gets room to roll.
- The mooring rope crosses the whole first part in one line and is in view from the first step.
- The two eggs sit at the bottom and the top of the zone. Both need a move that goes against the climb.
- The gate at the top faces the next zone, so the slice ends with a view of what comes next.
