# B3 Spring Court

Map: `b03.svg`. Ticket: #30. Shared numbers and assumptions: `README.md`.

## Intent

The third level teaches the Coil Spring, with bumpers and moving platforms. The player uses one spring in a safe place, then enters a wide walled court that works like a pinball table. Two balconies and a tower stand round the court. The player chooses how to reach each one: spring, gear stair or carriage.

Flavour: a brass court under a clock tower. Two carriages circle the tower like the hands of a clock.

## Routes

**First run (white).** Take the spring and use it to reach the Step and its gem. Drop into the court and take three gems among the bumpers. Spring up for the sky gem and land back in the court. Spring onto the west balcony for a gem. Ride a carriage past the north dock to the east balcony for a gem. Ride on to the south dock, jump up to the tower and finish.

**Expert (dashed).** Take the court gems in one curve that ends at the west spring. Spring once, pass through the sky gem at the top of the arc and land on the west balcony: 18.6 m in 1.63 s needs 11.4 m/s at take-off. Drop back, cross the court, and spring to the east balcony. Take the gem and the balcony spring, and leap the 8 m gap to the tower (1.95 s in the air, so 4.1 m/s is enough). The expert never waits for a carriage.

## Gems: 7

- 1 on the Step. It is the reward for the first spring, and the player sees it from the start pad.
- 3 on the court floor, each in a pocket between bumpers. A wrong line costs a bounce, not a fall.
- 1 sky gem at +8.5 m over the north-west of the court. Only a spring reaches it.
- 1 on each balcony.

## Clockwork Egg

The back ledge is 3 m below the north dock. The tower hides it from the court and from both balconies. A player who rides a carriage sees it for the 2 s that the carriage waits at the north dock. Drop from the carriage onto the ledge. The spring on the ledge returns the marble to the tower. Players who skip the carriages never see it.

## Hazards and objects

Start pad, finish pad, 7 gems, 1 egg, 3 checkpoints, 5 Coil Springs, 7 bumpers, 2 moving platforms, 6 turning gears.

## Checkpoints

- **Checkpoint 1** on the Step, after the first spring.
- **Checkpoints 2 and 3** on the west and east balconies.

The court is walled, so a bumper cannot push the marble out of the level. A fall from a balcony lands in the court. A fall from a carriage goes into the gap between the court and the tower, and returns the marble to the last balcony.

## Why it is fun

- The first spring is a pure reward: one button, 10 m of height, a gem.
- The court is open and forgiving. Bumpers make a bad line funny, not fatal.
- Each high place has more than one way up, and the player sees all of them from the Step.
- The spring arc through the sky gem turns two tasks into one move. It is hard to aim and easy to understand.

### Numbers of the built level

- A spring lifts the marble 10 m. To land on a balcony from the court the marble needs about 5 m/s at the use and 1.63 s in the air; the expert arc through the sky gem needs 12 m/s.
- The gear stair: six gears 4.2 m across, steps of 1 m, gaps of 0.8 to 1.6 m between the teeth. Each gear turns once in 10 s, every second one clockwise. A jump at 3.5 m/s toward the next gear, from 1 m out from the middle of a gear, lands on the next one.
- A carriage moves at 4.5 m/s on average and 7.1 m/s at most, and stops 2 s at each of its six stops. A marble at rest rides through every stop.
- A marble that rolls off a carriage at a walk falls into the gap round the tower and respawns at the last balcony. One that leaves the south leg fast clears the court's north parapet and lands in the court.
- Out of bounds is at −8 m.
