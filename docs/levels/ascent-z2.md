# Ascent 2: The Broken Tower

Map: `ascent-z2.svg`. Ticket: #32. Shared numbers and rules: `README.md`. Heights: 150 to 300 m. The zone starts on the crown of the tower, where zone 1 ends.

## Intent

The top of the clock tower has broken into pieces that float in the air. The player climbs through the clock's own parts: its face, its gears, its mainspring, its bells and at last its spire. The mechanics in focus are moving surfaces and Steam Vents in chains. Two wide floors catch falls: the tilted clock face at the bottom and the Dial ring in the middle.

## Routes

**First run (white).** Gears, Mainspring ramp, stair shards, half the Dial ring, roof slabs, spire ramp. Every surface is wide, and the player learns to wait for a moving part.

**Expert (dashed).**

1. Straight up the clock face between the hands.
2. The vent chain without a landing: enter the first vent with about 3.5 m/s of drift, so that each lift ends over the next vent.
3. The pendulum rod and its three vents.
4. Half the Dial ring at full speed, then the chain of four vents.
5. The loose slope and the lightning rod.

A second trick is on the lift gear: jump from tooth to tooth up the rising side. The teeth are 1.2 m apart. This halves the 8 s ride.

## Gems

Ascent has no gems.

## Clockwork Egg (1 of 10)

The hub of the clock face has a hole where the axle was. The hands hide it as they pass. A marble that drops into the hole lands in the movement chamber under the face, where the egg sits. A small vent in the chamber lifts the marble back onto the face. The cost is one more crossing of the face.

## Hazards and objects

15 Steam Vents (plus 1 in the egg chamber), 4 pendulums (walkway, mainspring bell, two slab bells), 4 turning gears and the 2 clock hands (turning arms), 3 moving platforms, 1 strip of Crumbling Tiles (slate slope), 1 narrow beam (lightning rod), 1 egg. No carried power-ups.

## Why it is fun

- The clock face is a big open slope with moving hands. No two crossings are the same.
- Every part of the zone moves on a clock, so a skilled player can learn the timing and never wait.
- The Dial ring is a long fast roll in the middle of a climb, with the whole zone in view.
- The vent chains are three and four launches in a row. A clean chain feels like flight.
- The last move of the expert route is a narrow rod to the top of the spire.

### Routes and times

The route bot drives both routes (`just test-levels zone_2`): first run **169.1 s**, expert **73.1 s**. Par 253.8 s and gold 84.1 s come from `just suggest-times zone_2`. The bot is slower than the estimates (112 s and 59 s): it waits for each mover's window, rolls the mainspring at 7 m/s in the outer lane past the bell, and loops round the spire vent at 3 m/s. A route has at most 100 points (`kit.marker`), so the first run has no point of its own on each shard step: the bot jumps where it crosses a step's jump point. Both routes start on the start pad, so the bot reaches the first jump from the same side in the climb as in the zone on its own.

### Climb with zone 1

A temporary GUT test (not in the repository) drove the route bot through a new Ascent climb of zones 1 and 2, with zone 1's route and then zone 2's, from the climb start: expert then expert reaches the gate on the cap in 188.7 s, expert then first run in 281.2 s, both with no fall. First run then first run reaches the crown with no fall and climbs to +243 m with no fall, where the bot's limit of 300 s of run time (`MAX_SECONDS` in `game/harness/route_bot.gd`) ends it.

### Clockwork Egg as built

The egg stands in the movement chamber under the dial, 0.9 m from the hub hole. The marble drops through the hub hole (radius 0.5 m) and lands beside it. A vent (radius 0.5 m) under the winding hole at four o'clock (radius 0.85 m) lifts the marble back onto the face. Tested by hand in the harness: drop, egg found, vent out onto the face. `docs/secrets.md` has the row.

### Look

Floors are kit limestone with a chamfered, dressed coping; sides and broken masses are coursed ashlar. Big brass parts (dial ring, barrel lid, rods, kerbs, the hub boss) use `polished_brass` (Metal007, metallic). The mainspring and the hands use `blued_steel` (Metal008, a deep blue). The roof slabs, the spire and its ramp are slate. No AI art and no props are used.
