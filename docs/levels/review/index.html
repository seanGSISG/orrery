Orrery M1 level layouts

Six play spaces. Each one has open floors, at least two routes and an expert line. White is a first run. Dashed pink is the expert route.

B1 Dawn Plaza

  • Focus: rolling, the camera, gems, the finish. No hazards.
  • Space: a 34 × 26 m plaza with a terrace (+2 m), a garden (−2 m) and an upper court (+4 m).
  • Choice: 8 gems on four floors, in any order.
  • Expert: drop into the garden, then cross the Minute Hand beam. One loop, no way back.
  • Numbers: par 41 s, gold 21 s. Egg in a vault that only the camera finds.

B2 Loose Hours

  • Focus: jumping and checkpoints, with trapdoors and crumbling tiles.
  • Space: a safe jump yard, then a 40 × 16 m hall over a drop: a grid of 40 cells, with 37 floor panels and 3 holes.
  • Choice: go round on solid panels or straight across the loose ones. Then three climbs: safe stair, precise pillars, fast crumbling ramp.
  • Expert: one line through the hall at 8 m/s or more, a jump over a 4 m hole, then the crumbling ramp.
  • Numbers: 6 gems, 2 checkpoints, par 48 s, gold 20 s. Egg under a trapdoor, with a glint through the floor gaps as a hint.

B3 Spring Court

  • Focus: the Coil Spring, with bumpers and moving platforms.
  • Space: a walled 44 × 29 m bumper court below two balconies and a tower.
  • Choice: reach each high place by spring, by gear stair or by carriage.
  • Expert: one spring arc through the sky gem onto the west balcony, a second spring to the east balcony for its gem, then a spring leap to the tower. No waiting.
  • Numbers: 7 gems, 3 checkpoints, par 74 s, gold 26 s. Egg on a ledge that only a carriage ride shows.

Ascent 1: Clockmaker's Square

  • Focus: how Ascent works: step jumps, Steam Vents, moving platforms. 0 to 150 m.
  • Space: an open square, roofs on two sides, and a clock tower with five floors.
  • Choice: market side or workshop side to the tower. Then on every floor: the inside way (safe) or the outside way (fast).
  • Expert: the crane beam, a ride on the counterweight, a weave through three vents, two loose slopes, two rafters to the crown.
  • Numbers: first run 122 s, expert 67 s. 1 egg, down a dry chimney.

Ascent 2: The Broken Tower

  • Focus: moving surfaces and chains of Steam Vents. 150 to 300 m.
  • Space: a tilted clock face 21 m across and a flat Dial ring 50 m across. Both catch falls.
  • Choice: five parts, each with a west way and an east way. They cross on the Dial ring.
  • Expert: vent chains of three and four with no landing, then a narrow lightning rod to the top of the spire.
  • Numbers: first run 112 s, expert 59 s. 1 egg, through the hub of the clock face.

BriefMap file

Ascent 3: Sky Harbour

  • Focus: every M1 object, in longer chains between rest ledges. 300 to 500 m.
  • Space: five big roofs and seven airships with wide backs.
  • Choice: six parts, each with a slow safe way on platforms and a fast way with no waits.
  • Expert: four mooring ropes, the crow's nests, then the outside of the Flagship over loose slates.
  • Numbers: first run 152 s, expert 73 s. 2 eggs: under the first airship and on the last fin.

Decisions, 2026-11-20

  1. B1, B2, B3: build as drawn.
  2. Par and gold: par = first-run route time × 1.5. Gold = expert route time × 1.15, so gold needs the expert line. Each level script carries both routes.
  3. Ascent: zones are 0 to 150 m, 150 to 300 m and 300 to 500 m. Zones 1 to 3 take about 6.4 minutes on the first-run routes and 3.3 minutes on the expert routes.
  4. Coil Spring: 20 m/s launch, about 10 m high. The pickup returns after 5 s.
